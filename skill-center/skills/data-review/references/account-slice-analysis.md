# Account Slice Analysis

Use this reference when the review must go beyond one post and judge the platform account as a moving system.

## What account-level analysis should answer

- is the current post weak, or is the whole account currently weak on this platform
- is the account improving, flat, or declining over recent comparable posts
- is the problem content-level, format-level, timing-level, or account-state-level
- should tomorrow's action focus on a new topic, a packaging retest, or account repair

## Default comparison windows

Unless the user gives a narrower or wider scope, compare in this order:

1. current batch
2. last 3 comparable posts on the same platform and format
3. last 7 days if enough posts exist
4. last 30 days only when the account is active enough to make it meaningful

Comparable means:

- video vs video
- article vs article
- answer vs answer
- same account and same platform first

## Signals to collect across recent history

### Distribution floor

Look for:

- how many recent posts got near-zero distribution
- whether `已发布` regularly turns into real reach
- whether late-night posts are consistently weak or just one-off

### Volatility

Look for:

- one spike surrounded by weak posts
- one weak post surrounded by normal posts
- large swings that suggest unstable route or inconsistent packaging

### Format fit

Look for:

- video repeatedly weak while article repeatedly okay on the same topic family
- one platform preferring answer / article over short video, or the reverse

### Audit and route stability

Look for:

- repeated `审核中`
- repeated draft-only outcomes
- repeated missing descriptions, duplicate blocks, role issues, captcha, SMS, or permission problems

### Topic fatigue or pattern fatigue

Look for:

- multiple posts using the same framing but getting progressively weaker
- repeated titles or hooks that no longer earn entry
- audience comments showing the format feels repetitive or too familiar

## How to interpret the account slice

### One-post miss, account still normal

Signs:

- recent baseline is normal
- only the current post is weak
- no recurring moderation or route issue exists

Diagnosis:

- likely a hook, title, cover, or structure miss

Action:

- retest one packaging variable

### Account cooling down

Signs:

- several recent posts are all near the low end
- not just one topic family, but multiple recent items are weak
- no single packaging fix fully explains the pattern

Diagnosis:

- account distribution or audience response is softening

Action:

- reduce forced volume
- test one sharper topic and one stronger packaging reset
- verify route, permissions, and moderation before blaming content alone

### Format mismatch on the platform

Signs:

- article/answer repeatedly outperforms short video on the same platform, or the reverse
- current post is weak in the weaker format but normal in the stronger format

Diagnosis:

- platform-account fit is format-specific, not universal

Action:

- shift tomorrow's weight toward the stronger format
- keep the topic but change the delivery form

### Operations drag disguised as content weakness

Signs:

- repeated delayed review
- recurring missing fields after publish
- frequent login or verification breaks
- creator-center confirmation lags or route instability

Diagnosis:

- the account's publishing route is distorting performance

Action:

- repair route first
- do not Cut the topic from these signals alone

## Account-level decision language

Use short, explicit judgments such as:

- account steady, single-post miss
- account warming, keep testing
- account weak, reduce volume and repair route
- account format mismatch, shift to article
- account evidence insufficient
