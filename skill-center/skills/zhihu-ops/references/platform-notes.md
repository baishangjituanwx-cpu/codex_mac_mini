## Official Sources

### 创作手册

Sources:
- https://www.zhihu.com/knowledge-plan/manual
- search snapshot observed on 2026-04-02

Key points:
- The official creator manual positions `回答`, `文章`, `想法`, and `视频` as distinct content forms.
- It points creators toward recommended questions, active topics, and search as demand discovery surfaces.
- The manual emphasizes:
- trusted sources
- clear structure
- polished language
- image use as supporting material
- It also warns against title bait and hard marketing, and points to moderation tools such as reporting low-quality information and handling comment quality.

Sources:

- https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/665741218

Key points:
- Official community guidance for organization-style operations stresses a `严肃、专业、友善` tone.
- High-risk behaviors called out in official materials include:
- plagiarism or stitched aggregation
- unrelated or decorative images
- hard ads and obvious diversion
- fake credentials or fake identity
- data tampering or fake interaction
- overly刺激 or misleading packaging

Key points:
- The official incentive material references `创作中心` and `收益分析`.
- It highlights content quality, interaction data, reading volume, vertical consistency, post count, and posting frequency as important signals.
- It also notes that diversion or promotional violations against community norms can disqualify incentive participation.

## Operating Guidance

- Format choice is an operational decision on 知乎, not just a cosmetic one.
- When the content is trust-sensitive, prefer stronger sourcing and slower claims over faster posting.
- Use comments as qualitative research: confusion means structure may be weak, hostility without substance often means avoid escalation, and repeated questions signal content gaps.
