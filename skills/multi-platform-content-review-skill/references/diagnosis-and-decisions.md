# Diagnosis And Decisions

Separate the cause before you prescribe the next move.

## Diagnose these dimensions separately

### Hook weakness

Common signs:

- users enter but drop early
- average watch time or completion is weak
- comments imply "too slow", "not clear", or "point came too late"

Next move:

- rewrite the first 1 to 3 seconds
- put the pain, judgment, or payoff earlier
- shorten setup before method or proof

### Title weakness

Common signs:

- impressions or feed exposure exist but opens are weak
- title is vague, generic, or mismatched with the actual content
- comments say the promise and the content do not match

Next move:

- tighten the promise
- use a clearer question, conflict, or business-problem frame
- match the real payoff instead of writing a broad slogan

### Cover weakness

Common signs:

- feed exposure exists but click or play-start is weak
- the cover looks generic, low-contrast, or visually unfocused
- cover text is too long or does not carry one clear promise

Next move:

- use a stronger face frame or scene
- keep the theme text short and specific
- clarify the conflict, cost, or result in one glance

### Content-structure weakness

Common signs:

- users enter, then drop before the method or proof arrives
- read depth is weak even though title and cover earned entry
- comments say the content rambles, repeats, or stays abstract

Next move:

- reorder to pain -> judgment -> method -> proof -> CTA
- cut filler and repeated setup
- move the concrete example earlier

### Account-state or moderation issue

Common signs:

- status shows `审核中`, limited distribution, or unusual visibility
- the platform gives little reach despite normal packaging
- moderation warnings or account-health issues appear

Next move:

- treat it as an operations issue first
- verify management-list visibility and moderation state
- do not blame the topic until account-state is cleared

### Login or publish-route breakage

Common signs:

- the editor looked successful but the management list cannot confirm publication
- the content is only saved as draft
- the platform shows duplicate, permission, captcha, SMS, or role blocker

Next move:

- fix the route and re-verify
- do not count this as a real content loss
- report the exact blocker text when available

## Keep / Cut / Retest

### Keep

Use when:

- the post shows strong relative signal for that platform role, or
- comments or DMs reveal real trust, demand, or conversion intent, or
- one platform proves the topic and only minor polish is needed elsewhere

Action:

- keep the core topic
- amplify the winning angle
- tune only secondary packaging variables

### Cut

Use when:

- the topic or framing repeatedly underperforms across multiple platforms
- comments show low relevance, wrong audience, or no practical demand
- no clear packaging fix explains the failure

Action:

- stop this angle for tomorrow
- preserve any small reusable hook or phrase
- move to a new topic

### Retest

Use when:

- one main variable likely caused the miss
- the topic still shows demand or trust signal
- the data is too early or incomplete for a hard cut

Action:

- change one primary variable first: hook, title, cover, structure, or publish route
- state exactly what will change on the next attempt

## Fast pattern checks

- impressions low + moderation label => account or moderation issue
- impressions okay + opens low => title or cover issue
- opens okay + watch or read depth poor => hook or structure issue
- modest traffic + strong comments or DMs => keep or retest; do not cut too early
- high traffic + low-value noise only => packaging mismatch or wrong audience
- platform says published but management list cannot confirm => publish-route issue

## Minimum return for each platform

- strongest signal
- biggest problem
- tomorrow's action
- Keep / Cut / Retest
- one sentence explaining why that decision fits
