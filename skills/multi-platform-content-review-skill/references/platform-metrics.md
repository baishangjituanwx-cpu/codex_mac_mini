# Platform Metrics

The goal is a same-day or next-morning first-wave review, not a fake universal score.

## Default platform matrix

Use the real publish package first. If the package is missing, fall back to this default matrix:

- 视频号: video
- 抖音: video
- 快手: video
- B站: video
- 微博: video or mixed post
- 百家号: video or article
- 知乎: video, answer, or article
- 头条号: video or article

## Evidence priority for every post

1. publish state
2. distribution or reach
3. click or open
4. consumption depth
5. interaction quality
6. trust or conversion signal
7. moderation or operations blocker

## Video content

Prefer the exact platform wording when available. Group the evidence like this:

- reach: plays, views, impressions, cover exposure
- entry: cover click-through, open rate, play-start rate
- consumption: average watch time, 3-second hold, completion, read-through of subtitles if visible
- interaction: likes, comments, shares, favorites, reposts
- business signal: profile visits, follows, DMs, explicit lead comments

Use these platform-specific priorities:

- 视频号: plays, comments, shares, follows, trust comments, private-domain-friendly shares
- 抖音: plays, completion or average watch time, likes, comments, shares, profile visits
- 快手: plays, comments, likes, practical feedback, plain-language response quality
- B站: plays, comments, favorites, coins, shares, audience quality
- 微博视频: plays, comments, reposts, discussion quality, public-opinion spread
- 百家号视频: plays, interactions, recommendations, comments
- 知乎视频: plays,赞同,收藏,评论, trust questions
- 头条号视频: 播放, 展现, 评论, 收藏, 转发, feed distribution

## Article or long-form content

Prefer:

- reach: impressions, recommendations, feed exposure
- entry: opens, read starts, title click performance
- consumption: read depth, average read time, completion if visible
- interaction: likes, comments, favorites, collects, reposts
- business signal: trust questions, saves, follows, DMs, explicit demand

Use these platform-specific priorities:

- 百家号图文: 阅读, 展现, 点赞, 评论, 收藏, 搜索型长尾信号
- 知乎回答或文章: 阅读, 赞同, 收藏, 评论, 关注, trust-building quality
- 头条号图文: 展现, 阅读, 评论, 收藏, 转发, problem-framing fit
- 微博图文: 阅读, 评论, 转发, public discussion quality

## Metric handling rules

- Write raw numbers exactly as seen before adding interpretation.
- Distinguish `0`, `not visible`, `pending review`, and `not checked`.
- Compare within the same format and the same time window before calling winners and losers.
- If a post published late at night, it is acceptable to review it on the next morning and state that timing explicitly.
- If the platform does not expose a metric, do not infer it from other fields unless the user explicitly asks for estimation.
